# Compass, Redfin, and Zillow: downloadable data and offline product opportunities

Research date: October 2, 2026, America/Los_Angeles. Sources below are current official pages unless identified as local repository evidence. This is product/data diligence, not a legal opinion. Anonymous access and technical downloadability do not establish redistribution rights.

Follow-up: [ZTRAX eligibility and actual Redfin download verification](ztrax-access-and-redfin-downloads.md) establishes the commercial/unaffiliated research exclusions, parses the legacy national file, and records limitations of the current official CSV download attempt. Those newer findings supersede tentative ZTRAX eligibility language below.

## Findings that change the existing assumptions

- **Zillow Research is useful for offline market statistics, not individual-home listings or transaction rows.** Existing RealtyKit CSV ingestion is the correct technical foundation. Its official developer catalog expressly permits public use with clear attribution, subject to its published terms; commercial and competing-product rights still need to be resolved for the intended app. [Official real estate metrics catalog](https://www.zillowgroup.com/developers/api/public-data/real-estate-metrics/).
- **Redfin's legacy S3 file and current Data Center are different delivery paths.** The repository's national S3 object remains dated June 2, 2026. The new download hub offers filtered CSVs and newer releases. Therefore “Redfin is stale” is too broad; the existing adapter is stale. [Current download hub](https://www.redfin.com/news/data-center/downloads/).
- **ZTRAX is available again through ICPSR.** Its 2023 withdrawal is historical; distribution resumed in 2026. Restricted research access does not make it an unrestricted commercial data feed. [ICPSR announcement, April 10, 2026](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/sites/icpsr/news/7-things-to-know-about-the-ztrax-database-at-icpsr).
- **Compass publishes reports, but this investigation found no documented public self-service listing API or machine-readable bulk export.** This is a search finding, not proof that partnership feeds cannot exist. [Compass research newsroom](https://www.compass.com/newsroom/research).

## What the data measures and where it originates

Zillow ZHVI estimates the value of the typical home stock rather than the median of homes that sold. Its neural valuation inputs include county public records, MLSs, brokerages, agents, and household information. Do not present ZHVI as the price of a specific closed home. [ZHVI methodology](https://www.zillow.com/research/zhvi-methodology/).

ZORI is based on repeated asking-rent observations and weights them to reflect the rental stock. It is not a signed-lease database or evidence of an individual tenant's rent. [ZORI methodology](https://www.zillow.com/research/methodology-zori-repeat-rent-27092/).

Zillow's housing download catalog contains prices/rents, forecasts, inventory, sales aggregates, listing behavior, market heat, new construction and affordability. Crucially, it says weekly observation cuts are CSVs updated monthly; sales nowcasts account for reporting latency. This is a publication clock distinct from the observation frequency. [Housing download definitions](https://www.zillow.com/research/data/).

Redfin's tracker uses rolling four-week weekly windows; monthly city/ZIP/neighborhood figures use rolling three-month windows. It revises late-reported transactions, and the newer pipeline distinguishes seasonal adjustment and raw values. Its buyer/seller model combines MLS inputs with proprietary search-time observations. [Redfin methodology](https://www.redfin.com/news/data-center/methodology/).

Redfin expanded downloadable metrics in May 2026, including purchase cancellations, price drops, financing, starter/luxury market data and delisting/relisting behavior. Counts changed when the pipeline switched to direct coverage aggregation and four-week totals. Historical comparisons must retain methodology/version metadata. [May 2026 redesign announcement](https://www.redfin.com/news/new-redfin-data-center/).

Compass's current Northern California research covers Santa Clara, San Mateo, San Francisco, Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, Napa, Sonoma, Solano and other submarkets. It offers segmentation ideas, not an established CSV source. [September 2026 Bay Area report](https://www.compass.com/newsroom/press-releases/1WFq1F1inC6ezxL2Zm8Jq9). Its listing disclosures identify regional sources including SFAR, MLSListings, BAREIS, OLR and Brooklyn MLS, with personal-use and redistribution restrictions. [Compass MLS disclosures](https://www.compass.com/legal/mls-disclosures/).

## Offline retention and public display: precise unresolved boundaries

**Zillow Research:** the official real-estate-metrics page permits public use and requires clear attribution, while the broader Market Data page also describes free public use. Those statements support research downloading; they do not clearly resolve a commercial competing app's automated refresh, raw redistribution, or database redistribution rights. Neither page reviewed publishes a concrete retention duration. [Metrics catalog](https://www.zillowgroup.com/developers/api/public-data/real-estate-metrics/), [Market Data](https://www.zillow.com/research/market-data/).

**Zillow general terms:** updated October 28, 2025, section 4(C) permits derivative displays of the specified Local-Info Aggregate Data with “Data Provided by Zillow Group” on every display page, with no logos or implied partnership. It does not unambiguously name the Research CSVs. Section 5 contains restrictions on automation, competitive products and links, so source-specific commercial confirmation is warranted. [Zillow terms](https://www.zillow.com/corporate/terms-of-use/).

**Zillow legacy API terms:** `/developers/terms/` describes ZWSID-era APIs, archived display rules and a 2006–2014 copyright notice. It forbids retained API copies and bulk user access and specifies 1,000 daily calls for its Home Valuation/Property Details APIs. These are not evidence of the current Bridge plan's quota, nor a CSV research-data retention rule. Obtain the actual current contract for the selected product. [Published legacy API terms](https://www.zillowgroup.com/developers/terms/).

**Current Zillow/Bridge API terms:** the platform links [Zillow API Terms](https://bridgedataoutput.com/zillowterms), rendered and read in the browser. Sections 5.6/5.14 require written permission for app implementation/distribution and competing products; sections 5.18/6 restrict end-user downloads/caching; section 11 requires deletion on termination. Section 25 restricts foreign export/hosting. Section 27 requires economic-data logo/link attribution, “Provided via the Zillow Economic Data API,” and an as-is notice; its quota is 5,000 calls **per day** unless approved otherwise. Section 28 limits public records to 1,000/day and single-record redistribution, and restricts homeowner/financial personal data to confidential internal employee use. No general operator cache duration was verified. These current API terms are specific to API-sourced data and do not establish the Research CSV license.

**Redfin:** updated September 29, 2025, sections 2.3.3–2.3.5 restrict reproduction/redistribution/commercial exploitation, third-party display, and automated querying without written permission, while allowing service-permitted actions. The Data Center intentionally offers research downloads, but reviewed current hub/methodology pages do not supply a clear dataset-specific commercial public-display license or retention period. Attribution is prudent but does not, by itself, cure the missing grant. Consumer home-table exports inherit MLS limits. [Redfin terms](https://www.redfin.com/about/terms-of-use).

**Compass:** July 27, 2026 terms, sections 5–7, restrict listing use, harvesting, derivative works, transfer, and unlicensed use of content/trademarks. Reports being readable/downloadable does not imply permission to host their charts, images, or database in RealtyKit. [Compass terms](https://www.compass.com/legal/terms-of-service).

**ZTRAX/ICPSR:** restricted file application and institutional research conditions must control approved storage/output. The actual restricted-use agreement was not obtained; do not assume a commercial app qualifies. Semiannual updates and absence of listing/rental data also make it a poor “recently closed / available now” product foundation. [ICPSR access explanation](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/sites/icpsr/news/7-things-to-know-about-the-ztrax-database-at-icpsr).

## Licensed route for property cards, live listings and individual closed sales

Bridge's public-record API is explicitly aimed at commercial uses; its MLS API requires the individual MLS's approval. Request the target region list, fields/statuses, closed-history depth, cost, bulk replication entitlement, cache duration, termination deletion, attribution, image rights and redistribution scope. A credential alone is not enough.

Current Bridge documentation explains that MLS approvals independently enable replication and specify feed types (IDX/VOW/BBO), field restrictions and suppression. A production ingestion should retain those permissions and use server tokens. Zillow's separate economic/public-record approvals and restrictions must not be replaced by MLS-feed assumptions. [Bridge platform documentation](https://bridgedataoutput.com/docs/platform/Introduction).

RESO supplies transport and field standards, not an MLS dataset. Access and licensing come from each MLS and its provider. Its Web API supports queries and replication, but replication capability is distinct from contractual permission. [RESO access FAQ](https://www.reso.org/reso-web-api/).

Official repositories reviewed as implementation references: [RESO Web API Core specification](https://github.com/RESOStandards/transport/blob/a99fb6ca307208280ac51bca1f573e89cb67b202/proposals/web-api-core.md) and [RESO Web API Commander](https://github.com/RESOStandards/web-api-commander). These are useful for schema/auth/query validation; they do not confer property-data rights. The ICPSR article links research import tooling through its study portal; that portal's exact restricted agreement and repositories were not accessible during this check.

## Recommendations grounded in RealtyKit's existing code

1. **Add buyer negotiation context:** market price-cut share, sold-above-list share, cancellation rate and listing withdrawal/relisting trends. Prefer aggregate dashboard cards with source dates after display rights are confirmed. Current Redfin exports make local/date-bounded ingestion plausible; do a small schema spike before expanding its adapter.
2. **Expand city comparison and rent-versus-buy:** extend existing ZHVI/ZORI ingestion to NY/NJ and additional Bay Area cities, preserving missing coverage. User-adjustable financing/tax/insurance/maintenance assumptions make the result useful without pretending regional indices price individual properties.
3. **Upgrade the source ledger:** store observation interval, publication cadence, expected publication lag, revision/version, seasonal adjustment, license scope, allowed display fields and cache/deletion limits. Distinguish “latest expected release” from a universal seven-day requirement. A September inventory file with a September 19 observation should remain honestly dated; monthly production need not masquerade as live.
4. **Add geography fidelity:** do not silently use San Jose metro values as Palo Alto city values. Zillow city ZORI is already filtered to four Bay Area cities; broader aggregate metric support can expose exact fallback labels.
5. **Separate public indicators from listing history:** keep ZORI out of the individual rental-observation import table, and keep ZHVI/Redfin median sales out of closed-sale property records. Extend the existing licensed RentCast/MLS path for property-level data.
6. **Treat provider permissions as data:** gate public exports and rich cards on documented source rights. This is a concrete capability record, not an inference from a successful HTTP response or a source name supplied in a local CSV.

Repository evidence: `realtykit/providers/zillow_research.py` already caches/melts regional CSVs and derives changes; `redfin_research.py` only handles national legacy TSV with a 20 MiB cap and forces stale; `compass.py` is an unavailable stub. `docs/historical-rentals.md` defines local, loopback-only rental imports and ZORI separation. `README.md` states licensed listing intent and general provider-term review. Existing caches and imports are reusable, but these documents do not prove vendor authorization. No production adapter or app behavior was changed by this research.

# Official closed-home sale data: New York, New Jersey, and the Bay Area

Research date: **October 2, 2026** (America/Los_Angeles). This is acquisition research and bounded local NJ analysis, not an app ingestion run or a legal opinion. Four NJ sale ZIPs and federal index files were downloaded for the linked aggregate analysis; no account was created, no county was contacted, and no portal was scraped. Sources below are government publishers, their official hosted metadata, or the existing repository research where explicitly identified.

## Decision and changes from existing research

Use **NYC Department of Finance rolling sales** for the easiest free official sold-home pilot: its current monthly dataset has address, unit, parcel identity, price, date, and residential classification, with an official API/export surface. New Jersey's statewide **SR1A** files are the next strongest offline-data opportunity, and New York **SalesWeb** supplies the rest of that state. These support recorded-sale history and neighborhood comparisons, with disclosure of reporting delay. They cannot supply active listings or rentals.

The Bay Area is a county integration problem. A meaningful new lead is **Sonoma's official public parcel service**, whose schema exposes current and prior sale prices and recording dates. It needs sample validation and clarification of price derivation before calling it an exact-price feed. Napa publishes an affordable monthly sales-list subscription, but the product's fields and transport are unverified. Santa Clara and San Mateo remain county-request workflows for RealtyKit's existing South Bay focus.

Two corrections matter:

- Marin's published fee study says its transfer-list indicated sale price is derived from documentary transfer tax and can differ from actual purchase price. The existing recommendation in `docs/research/04-government-data.md` to make Marin the first exact-price county should be revisited. [Marin 2025 fee study, report pages 9–10](https://assets.marincounty.gov/arcc-prod/public/2025-06/User%20Fees%20Study%20Final%20Report_05292025.pdf)
- Santa Clara Recorder's “data sales reports” means selling index/document data. The Recorder explicitly says it lacks property addresses, asking versus selling prices, and other real-estate sales data. A subscription is not a sold-price feed. [Official subscription description](https://clerkrecorder.santaclaracounty.gov/official-records/subscribe-data-sales-reports)

### NYC pilot details

The Open Data catalog currently lists 82,345 rows and 21 columns. Refresh with a full bounded rolling-window snapshot, deduplicate across monthly versions, and keep annual files for older history. Do not infer that a newly fetched record closed this week. Use the publisher update date alongside sale date. [Current catalog metadata](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/dataset/NYC-Citywide-Rolling-Calendar-Sales/usep-8jbt)

Official API/export discovery starts at dataset `usep-8jbt`, conventionally `/resource/usep-8jbt.json` and `/api/views/usep-8jbt`; validate actual endpoint responses before implementation. Borough XLSX links are the documented fallback. Keep borough/block/lot **and apartment number**: a co-op unit must not collapse to a building parcel.

For residential comparisons, filter by building class/category at sale and residential use, rather than merely selecting tax class 1. Class 1 can include vacant residential land and mixed-use properties; class 2 includes larger residential buildings and co-ops. Zero-dollar transfers represent ownership changes without cash consideration. Positive prices alone do not prove arm's-length transactions. Flag nominal, mixed-use, multi-unit, and potentially partial-interest transfers. [DOF glossary](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/property/glossary-property-sales.page)

### NY outside NYC

SalesWeb's January 8, 2025 official FAQ was readable through the web search index; directly opening that page and its application returned tool errors. This establishes the state's documented download offering, not successful 2026 access or a current file observation date. Verify the live download/disclaimer workflow before scheduling acquisition. The FAQ excludes co-op transfers, which is material when comparing coverage with NYC DOF. [Official FAQ](https://www.tax.ny.gov/research/property/assess/sales/salesweb.htm)

The government-hosted SalesWeb dictionary defines the price as consideration after subtracting personal property. It includes arm's-length and assessor-review flags, property class, partial-parcel/condo indicators, and load/update dates. A subtle mapping issue: its `deed_date` means the date the county clerk recorded the deed, whereas `sale_date` is the transfer date shown on the deed. The dictionary is historical; validate current downloaded columns and encoding before adopting these mappings. [Official SalesWeb dictionary](https://swcf.orpts.ny.gov/cfapps/salesWebProd/salesWeb/datadict.pdf)

### NJ event filtering and parcel joins

Store reported and verified consideration separately; prefer verified where available and preserve its provenance. Preserve deed date versus recording date. U/N is a sales-ratio-study usability classification, not a universal exact arm's-length label; expose the reason code rather than treating all nonusable events as invalid. The 2024 assessor handbook says preliminary data is posted before screening, then final cumulative data is posted; assessors must return their determinations within three weeks of receiving sales information. This is a workflow interval, not a guarantee of public availability within three weeks of closing. [Assessor handbook, sections 1005.05–1005.06](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/pdf/assessorshandbook.pdf)

Join to NJGIN using county/municipality, complete block/lot including suffixes, and qualifier. NJ's parcel standard defines `PAMS_PIN` for common joins; decimal IDs and condominium qualifiers must survive normalization. Current hosted-service metadata and URLs matter: NJGIN retired a mature service in March 2026. [Parcel mapping standard](https://geoapps.nj.gov/njgin/parcel/NJGIS_ParcelMappingStandardv3.2.pdf), [NJGIN current notices](https://nj.gov/njgin/)

NJGIN's official download metadata lists a free zipped statewide file geodatabase at `https://geoapps.nj.gov/njgin/parcel/parcels_MOD4_Statewide.gdb.zip`, publication August 24, 2026, and redacted owner names. Geodatabase and hosted-service products should be treated as geometry/enrichment snapshots rather than timely sale-event sources. [NJOGIS metadata](https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/406cf6860390467d9f328ed19daa359d/info/metadata/metadata.xml?format=default&output=html)

### NJ county alternatives

County tax boards are the originating SR1A stewardship layer. Morris provides a public [SR1A search](https://mcweb1.co.morris.nj.us/McTaxBoard/SearchSR1A.aspx) with year and price filters; this is verified as a search interface, not as an authorized bulk export/API. It can validate targeted sample records. Monmouth's SR1A site was described as weekly in a 2018 government records decision, but that historical statement does not establish its 2026 cadence. [GRC decision](https://www.nj.gov/grc/decisions/pdf/2014-10.pdf)

For Bergen/Hudson/Essex/Middlesex and other counties, use the statewide SR1A file first rather than assume separate recorder searches are bulk feeds. If recent records are absent, request the tax board's current electronic SR1A extract, current schema and publication schedule, with buyer/seller names omitted. No county bulk-sale agreement or fresh county SLA was verified in this research.

## California and all nine Bay Area counties

No single official California statewide residential sale-event download/API was located. California RTC 408.1 establishes a county transfer-list route: two years of transfers, quarterly revision, APN/address/transfer and recording dates/reference, and consideration where known. Public inspection can cost at most $10; this does not cap a custom export/subscription price or guarantee an electronic API. Nonpublic change-of-ownership statement content is excluded. ### Sonoma validation plan

The schema explicitly distinguishes `SaleSalesPrice` and `SaleRecordingDate` from assessed-value fields, with matching prior-sale fields. It also includes `SaleTranserType` (publisher spelling), `SaleMultiParcel`, `MegabyteUpdated` and `last_edited_date`. A current parcel snapshot with two sale slots is not a complete deed-event archive; freeze snapshots if building history. [Official schema](https://socogis.sonomacounty.ca.gov/map/rest/services/CRAPublic/ParcelsPublic/MapServer/0)

The service describes monthly publishing, a public partial extract from the county tax system, privacy-related omission of owner names, and attribution required when distributing data. Some description text appears copied from a richer internal layer, so confirm the intended public-layer use limitations. [Service metadata](https://socogis.sonomacounty.ca.gov/map/rest/services/CRAPublic/ParcelsPublic/MapServer)

A one-row query restricted to sale metadata failed in the browser tool; a shell attempt failed DNS under the restricted environment. Consequently schema availability is verified, but **live row contents, missing-price rate, latest sale date, exact consideration versus tax-derived value, API reliability and terms are not validated**. Do not claim production readiness or statewide completeness. No bulk request was attempted.

## Product features these sources enable

1. **Recorded-sale map and comparable-sale explorer.** Filter by radius/time/property type and show source, transaction date, recording date, publisher date, price kind and quality badges. NYC is the first implementation candidate; NY/NJ import adapters follow. This is historical comparable evidence rather than active inventory.
2. **Neighborhood sale distributions.** Show medians, quartiles, count and price/area only for sufficiently comparable records. Require a minimum sample size, exclude nominal transfers from market summaries, and keep excluded records auditable. Aggregate geography must not suggest a single neighborhood row is an exact valuation.
3. **Property timeline.** Separate deed sales, parcel/assessment changes and licensed listing events. NYC unit identity, NJ qualifiers and California APNs make joins more reliable than addresses alone.
4. **Data-freshness and coverage panel.** Make missing prices, delayed reporting and county availability visible. A successful refresh does not make government transactions real time.
5. **Parcel, zoning and permit context.** NJGIN and county GIS sources add spatial detail independent of consumer listings. Contra Costa's official portal documents monthly map data and permit histories; permissions/schema for programmatic retrieval remain separate work.

## Acceptance criteria and remaining acquisition work

Start with a small NYC public-file/API pilot and build on the completed bounded NJ SR1A analysis; validate a Sonoma query sample. For RealtyKit's current South Bay geography, request Santa Clara's electronic list and San Mateo's product sample in parallel; NY/NJ readiness does not resolve California coverage.

- Verify downloaded file contents, checksum, data dictionary/version, latest transaction and publisher dates, and format/encoding before enabling a scheduled connector.
- Keep `sale_price`, `price_kind`, `sale_date`, `recorded_at`, `source_updated_at`, `fetched_at`, `source_url`, parcel/unit identity and quality flags distinct. Do not repurpose assessment values as prices.
- Check residential and arm's-length classification per publisher. Flag partial interests, multiparcel events, gifts/family transfers, foreclosure, missing consideration and derived prices.
- Omit buyer/seller/owner names and mailing addresses from the app's normalized store and API. Preserve NJ redactions and privacy obligations when researching future public display.
- Confirm storage, redistribution, attribution and automation terms for each transport. Public inspection, downloadable facts, paid file delivery and consumer listing/photo rights are different permissions.
- Validate at least 20 known closed-home examples across dates and property types before proposing a completeness or freshness SLA. Obtain county price-derivation definitions; Marin already demonstrates why a column named sale price is insufficient evidence.

No outreach, purchases, external publication, credentials, app import or implementation occurred. The linked NJ follow-up acquired bounded official files and calculated local aggregates.

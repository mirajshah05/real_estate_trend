# Competitors, feature opportunities, and repository reuse

Research reference date: October 2, 2026, America/Los_Angeles. Findings are from official product documentation, publisher research pages, and repository source/license files. Live pages can change; this is not an archived historical guarantee. No consumer listing scraper was run and no bulk property dataset was downloaded. Provider coverage statements below are claims by their publishers, not independently measured accuracy.

## Existing RealtyKit baseline

The README already documents city/ZIP/address search, geographic trends, stock/mortgage correlations, outliers, licensed-provider active listings and recorded sales, local rental CSV/JSON imports, source freshness, and dated government GIS provenance. These should not be described as new features. In particular, the app already distinguishes area aggregates from viewport properties, and county parcels from verified sales. Its loopback-only architecture and hard cap of 40 attempted RentCast calls/month favor cached regional research and user-triggered property investigation over continuous nationwide monitoring.

## What other apps offer and where their data originates

|---|---|---|---|
| Zillow | Property search, value/rent estimates, tax and price histories; BuyAbility translates income, down payment and a monthly budget into affordability with rate changes. | Zillow documents price history from direct MLS IDX feeds and tax assessment history from county public records aggregated by a third-party provider. Its research methodology distinguishes modeled ZHVI from observed-rent ZORI. [History sources](https://zillow.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/212564748-Where-does-Zillow-get-the-price-and-tax-history-data-for-my-home), [methodology](https://www.zillow.com/research/methodology/), [BuyAbility](https://www.zillow.com/learn/what-is-buyability/) | Build a transparent scenario calculator using existing rates and user inputs. ZHVI is an area/model measure, not an individual sale price. |
| Redfin | MLS search, public facts and past sales, saved-search price/status updates; market dashboards on price drops, cancellations, relistings, financing and affordability. | Redfin is a brokerage with local MLS feeds; its official data-quality page also identifies FSBO sources Fizber and FSBO.com. Public-record facts and MLS listing facts can differ. [Data quality](https://www.redfin.com/about/data-quality-on-redfin), [public records](https://support.redfin.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001454531-Public-Records), [Data Center](https://www.redfin.com/news/data-center/) | Add negotiation context and explicit source disagreements, without claiming that a public-record transfer is an MLS closing. |
| Compass | Collections organize a shortlist and facilitate comments, collaboration, and pricing/availability monitoring. Its private/coming-soon marketing creates another listing channel. | Regional MLS disclosures explicitly describe IDX/VOW sources and local display rules; private exclusives are Compass-controlled inventory. [Collections announcement](https://www.compass.com/newsroom/press-releases/4Oe2KpODpt9lUQtXlrJpe4/), [MLS disclosures](https://www.compass.com/legal/mls-disclosures/), [seller product](https://www.compass.com/sell/) | Add local saved research collections with user notes and outbound links. A visible Compass listing is not evidence that a reusable public dataset/feed exists. |
| Realtor.com | Search plus Research inventory, local market trends, demand/hotness analysis, and regional CSV histories. | Its Research Data Library explicitly says it aggregates its database of MLS-listed for-sale homes and hundreds of sources. CSVs include weekly national inventory, monthly national/state/metro/county/ZIP inventory, and metro/county/ZIP hotness. [Data Library](https://www.realtor.com/research/data/) | Strong incremental aggregate source for price reductions and market demand. Attribute Realtor.com Economic Research and link to its library as requested; review applicable use terms before product distribution. |
| Homes.com | Property search with substantial neighborhood, school and residential-building content. Market reports include closing prices, supply and sale-to-original-list ratio. | The official report identifies MLS providers, public records, and CoStar's own researchers; the product About page identifies in-house writers, researchers and photographers. [About](https://www.homes.com/about/), [market report and definitions](https://www.homes.com/reports/national-housing-market/) | Borrow the neighborhood-dossier concept using our own authorized public sources. Their proprietary writing, photos, research, and listings are not our dataset. |

[Integrations](https://www.apartments.com/grow/integrations), [unit content](https://propertyhelp.apartments.com/article/1019-unit-level-content-faqs), [About](https://www.apartments.com/grow/about) | Add effective-rent scenarios including mandatory fees and concessions when users or licensed sources provide them; asking rent is not the executed lease rent. |
| RentCast | Property facts/history, active sale/rental listings, value/rent estimates, and comparable-property searches. | Property records come from county records, recorded deeds and assessor databases. Records update approximately weekly, but recent transfers can lag weeks to months. [Property data](https://developers.rentcast.io/reference/property-data), [listing data](https://developers.rentcast.io/reference/property-listings) | Existing integration can support bounded, user-triggered comp investigation. Do not relabel its coverage claims as a full direct MLS feed; plan around the existing request cap. |
| PropStream | Public-record and MLS comps; failed listings; investor research and property lead lists. | Its public policy names government title/lien/court records and third-party MLS/data repositories. | DealCheck | Rental/BRRRR/flip calculations, financing/rehab/expense inputs, cash flow, cap rate, ROI, comps, offer ceilings, and sharable reports. | DealCheck documents property imports and an integration/API relationship with RentCast. [Product](https://dealcheck.io/), [integration help](https://help.dealcheck.io/en/collections/2978273-integrations) | Add scenario analysis and evidence exports; start with manual inputs and existing authorized facts. Do not treat investment outputs as assured returns. |

An important comparability issue: Homes.com defines days on market in its report through closing, while other series can measure time to pending. Realtor listing prices, Zillow modeled values, MLS closed-sale medians, HUD program rent benchmarks and submitted asking rents also measure different things. A combined chart should retain those definitions instead of averaging them into a single purported truth.

## Prioritized new feature ideas

The priorities below are product judgments, not claims by sources.

| Priority | Feature and user outcome | Inputs and implementation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Saved comparison workspace:** compare three homes or areas, retain notes, source links, dated evidence and decision criteria; export a portable research bundle. | Existing app records, user-entered addresses/URLs, and user notes. Keep original-source links. User-added URLs do not grant permission to fetch/copy photos or listing text. Local JSON export fits the current architecture; cloud collaboration would be a separate architecture change. |
| 1 | **All-in affordability and rent-versus-buy scenarios:** monthly ownership cost, cash required, break-even holding period, and rate/down-payment/tax sensitivity. | Existing FRED rates and housing metrics plus explicit user assumptions for taxes, insurance, HOA, maintenance, closing/selling costs, appreciation and rent growth. Show assumptions and ranges. Area-level prices are scenario defaults, not a subject-home valuation. |
| 1 | **Negotiation and liquidity panel:** price-reduction share, sale-to-list distribution, months of supply, and contract/cancellation context alongside existing inventory/DOM. | Extend existing Redfin integration and evaluate Realtor.com's downloadable aggregates. Confirm each downloadable series' geography, history and rights first; a dashboard's existence does not prove every widget has a CSV. Preserve changed definitions and revised observations. |
| 2 | **Explainable sold-comp workbench:** select/exclude comps with reasons; show type, size, distance, recency, source event and price evidence; expose mismatches rather than hiding them. | Existing RentCast sales plus regional official records from the companion government research. Use matching confidence and exclusion of nominal/non-market transfers. Labels should distinguish contract close date, deed execution date and recording date. Minimal API requests and cached/user-imported observations are necessary under the current cap. |
| 2 | **Rental evidence ladder and fee-adjusted rent:** compare local imported asking-rent observations, licensed comps, ZORI area trends, HUD FMR and Census occupied gross-rent statistics, with source-specific definitions. | [HUD FMR](https://www.huduser.gov/portal/datasets/fmr.html) offers API/history files and defines FMR as a 40th-percentile gross-rent benchmark. [ACS five-year API](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/data-sets/acs-5year.html) offers housing estimates down to fine geographies. Neither is a ledger of newly signed leases. This adds context to, rather than repeats, the existing rental-upload feature. |
| 2 | **Official property dossier:** parcel identity, permits, zoning and mapped hazards, with dates and original-record links. | County/city sources require jurisdiction-specific availability/terms checks. FEMA's NFHL is cataloged as geospatial data with an [official access endpoint](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/national-flood-hazard-layer). Do not interpret mapped flood zones as a comprehensive climate assessment or insurance quote. |
| 3 | **Access and commute explorer:** walking-network reach to transit, parks and chosen destinations, using map-derived routes with uncertainty. | OpenStreetMap plus OSMnx and separately licensed transit schedule feeds where appropriate. Keep OSM attribution/ODbL obligations and service usage policies; road network travel estimates are not live commute predictions. Avoid a generic neighborhood desirability score based on protected demographics. |

Recommended first product slice: saved local comparisons + all-in affordability + richer aggregate negotiation metrics. This creates useful new behavior without requiring an unrestricted live listing feed. Next, pick one geographic comp/dossier pilot where official transfer and parcel joins can be measured against an authorized provider sample. Outbound listing buttons still need provider-specific rules: [Realtor.com terms](https://www.realtor.com/terms-of-use/) require permission for deep links and written notice/attribution for homepage links. Its Research library's attribution/link instructions should be assessed within that specific research-data scope, not applied as blanket permission for consumer listings. See the companion legal note for other providers.

## Git repository findings

Repositories were inspected for README, relevant schema/client documentation or source, and actual license files. They were not installed or executed. These are reuse evaluations, not a security audit or verified build. Pin a commit and recheck its dependency licenses before incorporating code; default-branch pages are mutable.

| Repository | What was inspected / practical value | License and data-rights conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| [RESOStandards/reso-tools](https://github.com/RESOStandards/reso-tools) | Client SDK, OData query construction, OAuth2 credentials, metadata parsing, validation and reference/test-data server. [Client documentation](https://github.com/RESOStandards/reso-tools/tree/main/reso-client) is relevant to a future licensed MLS adapter; test-data generation can support fixtures. TypeScript SDK is not a drop-in Python backend provider. | The actual [root LICENSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RESOStandards/reso-tools/main/LICENSE) points to the [RESO EULA](https://www.reso.org/eula/), which grants defined rights for standards incorporation and includes restrictions. Do not label it MIT because the project describes itself as open-source. Confirm package-specific applicability before adoption. RESO is a transport/schema standard, not permission to access an MLS or redistribute its listings. |
| [openaddresses/openaddresses](https://github.com/openaddresses/openaddresses) | Source-discovery and normalization specifications. [Santa Clara source JSON](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openaddresses/openaddresses/master/sources/us/ca/santa_clara.json) maps official county address fields, APNs/parcels, building footprints and ESRI endpoints. Useful for adding jurisdictions and parcel/address joining. | Repository [LICENSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openaddresses/openaddresses/master/LICENSE) is BSD-3-Clause style. Upstream datasets still have their own terms/attribution; the examined county entry gives attribution but no explicit reusable dataset license. Verify the county publication rather than inferring rights. Address/parcel/building data is not sale history. |
| [gboeing/osmnx](https://github.com/gboeing/osmnx) | Network download/analysis and [routing module](https://github.com/gboeing/osmnx/blob/main/osmnx/routing.py); could support pedestrian access features. Adds a geospatial dependency stack and should be prototyped separately from current ingest. | Code is [MIT](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gboeing/osmnx/main/LICENSE.txt). Underlying [OpenStreetMap data is ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), with attribution and applicable database-sharing obligations; API, tile and Nominatim service policies are separate. |
| [censusdis/censusdis](https://github.com/censusdis/censusdis) | Python discovery, Census variables/geographies, download and mapping examples. Useful research guide for ACS housing/income/rent context. | Actual [LICENSE.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/censusdis/censusdis/main/LICENSE.md) is Hippocratic License 3.0 with use restrictions, not a conventional unrestricted permissive license. Prefer a small independent adapter using Census's documented API, or review the library license first. The [Census API terms](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/about/terms-of-service.html) explicitly address applications that display/analyze/retrieve its data. |
| [ZacharyHampton/HomeHarvest](https://github.com/ZacharyHampton/HomeHarvest) | README explicitly describes Realtor.com scraping, sale/rental/sold filters and CSV exports with MLS-like schemas. Do not integrate as the production feed or treat exported CSV as an authorized offline dataset. |

## Outstanding evidence and decisions

- The reviewed competitor pages establish data categories and acquisition relationships, not exact licensing prices or a right for RealtyKit to republish their consumer content.
- DealCheck's complete upstream field-level source chain and Apartments.com's complete record-level chain were not established. Avoid definitive vendor provenance claims beyond documented submission/integration paths.
- Test data from a repository cannot establish real county or MLS coverage. Measure omissions, price-field nulls, matching errors and recording lag in a geographically limited pilot.
- Government transfer availability and consumer linking/embedding permissions are addressed by the companion research notes. Those decisions should determine which property features can ship with original-source links, which require an API contract, and which remain unavailable.
